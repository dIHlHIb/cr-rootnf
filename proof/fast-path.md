# Rounding error in the fast path of cr_rootnf

This derivation applies to the floating-point expressions in [rootnf.c](../src/binary32/rootn/rootnf.c). It assumes binary32 and binary64 with dynamic rounding, correctly rounded fused multiply-add, no reassociation and no implicit contraction. The table checker uses 90-digit Decimal arithmetic. [Table checker](../tests/check_tables.py)

For a positive finite magnitude and `2 ≤ |n| < 2^32`, write the magnitude as `m·2^e`. Here `1 ≤ m < 2`, `−149 ≤ e ≤ 127` and conversion of the integer n to double is exact. Intermediate floating-point arithmetic is normal or zero. In any IEEE rounding direction each rounded operation on a normal result has relative error at most `u = 2^-52`.

1. The table reciprocals are integer multiples of `2^-20`. Hence `m·inv` and subtraction of 1 are exact. The checker bounds `|r| ≤ 0.003892` over both endpoints of every table cell. The omitted log series contributes less than `2^-57`. The omitted exponential series on `|f| ≤ 1/128` contributes less than `2^-54`. The checker also compares the table components and coefficients used by the code with 90-digit Decimal values, with a tolerance of `2^-52`.
2. For a conservative bound on the computed log polynomial: each pair `a + r·b` has magnitude below 2 and incurs at most `2u` rounding error. The errors in its inputs' coefficients contribute at most `(1+|r|)u`. The outer factors `r²` and `r⁴` are each smaller than 1, with absolute computation errors below `u` and `4u`. Summing these contributions and the outer rounding errors gives a deliberately loose `40u` absolute error in `p`. Multiplication by `|r| < 1/250` reduces this to less than `u`. Including the table value, FMA, truncation and addition of e gives an absolute error below `2^-44` in the computed log2. The last addition alone may cost `150u`.
3. Multiplication by a rounded reciprocal gives absolute error in `t` below `2^-43` since `|n| ≥ 2` and `|t| < 75`. Rounding `64t` to an integer and subtracting that exact multiple of 1/64 gives `|f| ≤ 1/128`. The subtraction is exact by Sterbenz's lemma, with the zero-multiple case immediate.
4. In the exponential polynomial the five coefficient errors are below `u` each, the intermediate pair magnitudes are below 1, and every outer multiplier has magnitude below 1. The sum of coefficient, evaluation and truncation errors in the small correction is below `32u`. The high table value is in [1,2). Including its error and the final FMA costs less than `80u` absolute in the unscaled exponential. The true unscaled magnitude exceeds 0.99. The exponential change caused by the error in t is at most `exp(ln(2)·2^-43)−1` relative. The combined relative error is below `2^-42`. Power-of-two rescaling is exact here.
5. Within a binary64 binade the float endpoints and midpoints occur at multiples of `2^28` bit steps. The fast path accepts only if its computed result is more than 8,192 binary64 steps from one of these boundaries on either side. That is at least `2^-40` times the computed magnitude. A possible power-of-two crossing also lands in the rejected strip. Accepted values can therefore be cast in the caller's mode after applying the sign.
6. For `|n| ≥ 2^32`, `|ln(x)/n| ≤ 149 ln(2)/2^32`. Exponentiating bounds the distance to 1 by less than `2^-25`. The direction from 1 follows from whether the magnitude is above 1 and from the sign of n. The same test works when the approximate value is within `2^-27` of 1 after allowing for the fast-path error. The proxy chooses the matching float cell for all four modes. Magnitude 1 is handled exactly beforehand.

An independent driver evaluated the same C expressions on 553,215 inputs in each rounding mode. The largest observed relative errors, expressed as base-two exponents, were −47.12 (nearest), −46.52 (upward), −46.55 (downward) and −46.10 (toward zero). [Test method](../tests/independent/README.md)

Within the guard, the implementation compares an integer power against a rounding boundary using outward-rounded products. [Integer comparison](NUMERICS.md)
